Exploring Heritage Sites: A Personal Perspective

Travel has always been more than just a break from work for me. It's a way to explore different cultures, understand architectural heritage, and gain fresh perspectives that often inform my approach to workplace design and facilities management.

Visiting heritage sites across India and beyond has taught me something valuable: the best spaces are those that blend history with functionality. Ancient temples, palaces, and traditional settlements have stood the test of time because they were built with an understanding of human needs, natural light, ventilation, and community.

These insights resonate deeply with modern CRE principles—sustainability, wellness, and user experience. When I walk through a centuries-old structure, I often find myself drawing parallels to contemporary workplace design: the importance of natural light, the value of communal spaces, and the need for environments that support both productivity and well-being.

If you get the chance, step outside your usual routine and explore. You might be surprised how much the past can inspire the future.
