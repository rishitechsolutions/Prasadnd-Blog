I am brought in to build them, stabilize them, and make them run without dependency.

Seventeen years. The environments change. The instinct does not.

My work sits at the intersection of strategy and execution translating what a business needs into operating models that deliver performance without overcomplication. I am not interested in complexity for its own sake. I am interested in what works, consistently, at scale, without constant intervention.

Simple enough to execute. Strong enough to grow. Built to run without me.

When I joined, a Corporate Real Estate function existed in India, but it had not been built into what the business actually needed.
